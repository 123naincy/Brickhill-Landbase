Location, sizes, payment plans, and travel time.

Where is the Sindhudurg project?

The project is in Nirvade, Sindhudurg, Maharashtra, close to the Goa border.

What plot sizes are offered?

Plots are planned from 350 to 450 square yards.

What payment plans are available?

Plans include 25:25:25:25, 50:50, and 90:10, plus booking assistance.

Goa Airport is about 30 minutes from the project. Film City is about 40 minutes, and beach shacks are about 25 minutes away.
