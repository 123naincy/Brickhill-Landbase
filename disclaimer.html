Website Disclaimer

This page explains what the website does and does not promise.

Brickhill Landbase publishes this website to introduce its listed projects. It is general information, not a sale offer, a legal opinion, or a promise of returns.

Images and amenities

Photographs and drawings, including the “Artistic Impression of Development” section, may be concepts. Amenities are proposed unless a sales person confirms in writing that an item is already built. Nearby places such as Tarkarli beach are not the plot itself.

Location

BHL Aaranya is in Nirvade, Sindhudurg, Maharashtra, near the Goa border. It is not a project in Goa. Drive times change with the route and traffic. Check Google Maps for Mopa or Dabolim before you rely on a travel time.

Prices, plans, and availability

Prices, plot sizes, and payment plans on this website are indicative. The master plan is a tracing of the layout drawing for browsing. Colours on that plan are sample availability, not a confirmed sales inventory. The registered agreement, not this website, sets the price, the payment dates, and what is included.

Documents

Ask for the papers for the specific plot before you pay. Those typically include the 7/12 extract where it applies, non-agricultural conversion status, layout approval, and an encumbrance check. BHL Aaranya in Nirvade, Sindhudurg is listed with MahaRERA registration no. P52900027782. Confirm that entry on maharera.maharashtra.gov.in. This number does not cover Eden Street, Bridal Square, or the Panipat plots, and this page does not state that every plot is already approved.

No guaranteed appreciation

Land values can rise or fall. Nothing on this website is a forecast or a guarantee of profit.

Other websites

Eden Street, Bridal Square, and the Panipat industrial plots open on group or partner websites. Those sites are responsible for their own details.
