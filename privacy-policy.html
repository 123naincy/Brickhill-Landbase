At Brickhill Landbase (“we,” “our,” or “us”), we respect your privacy and are committed to handling the personal information you share with us responsibly. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, store, disclose, and protect information when you visit our website, explore our real estate projects, or submit an enquiry about our properties and services.

01

Information We Collect

Depending on how you interact with our website, we may collect the following information:

Personal information

  • Your name, mobile number, and email address.
  • Your city, location, property preferences, or budget, when you choose to provide them.
  • Details and messages you submit through enquiry forms or other communication channels.

Technical and usage information

  • Browser type, device information, operating system, and IP address.
  • Pages viewed, referring URLs, and interactions with website content.
  • Information collected through cookies, analytics, or advertising technologies, where used.
02

How We Use Your Information

We may use your information to:

  • Respond to property enquiries and requests for information.
  • Share details about our residential, commercial, and other real estate projects.
  • Arrange site visits, consultations, and enquiry follow-ups.
  • Provide relevant updates about project details, pricing, availability, or developments.
  • Improve our website, customer service, and marketing performance.
  • Maintain business records and meet applicable legal obligations.
03

Sharing of Information

We do not sell your personal information. Where necessary for the purposes described in this policy, we may share relevant information with authorized team members, affiliated entities, CRM and technology providers, communication providers, or marketing and service partners.

We may also disclose information where required by applicable law, a lawful request from an authorized authority, or to protect our rights. Any sharing is intended to be limited to what is reasonably necessary and handled subject to applicable legal requirements.

04

Cookies & Tracking Technologies

Our website may use cookies, analytics tools, and advertising technologies to understand website usage, improve performance, and measure campaign effectiveness. These tools may help us understand which pages visitors view and how they interact with our website.

You can manage cookies through your browser settings. Disabling certain cookies may affect some website features. Where required by applicable law, consent will be obtained before using non-essential cookies or tracking technologies.

05

Marketing Communications

If you submit an enquiry, we may contact you by phone, SMS, email, or messaging services to respond to your request and provide relevant follow-up information, subject to applicable law and your communication preferences.

You can request to stop promotional communications by using available unsubscribe instructions or contacting us directly. We will handle communication preferences in accordance with applicable requirements.

06

Data Security

We take reasonable technical and organizational measures designed to protect personal information against unauthorized access, loss, misuse, alteration, or disclosure. However, no electronic transmission or storage method can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

07

Data Retention

We retain personal information for as long as reasonably necessary to respond to enquiries, provide our services, maintain business records, resolve disputes, and meet legal requirements. When information is no longer needed, we will take appropriate steps to delete or anonymize it, subject to applicable law.

08

Your Privacy Rights

Depending on applicable law, you may be able to request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal information; withdraw consent where processing is based on consent; opt out of promotional communications; or raise a grievance about how your information is handled.

To make a request, contact us using the details below. We may need to verify your identity before processing certain requests.

09

Third-Party Websites

Our website may link to third-party websites or services. Their privacy practices and content are outside our control. We encourage you to review the privacy policy of any third-party website before sharing personal information with it.

10

Children's Privacy

Our website and property enquiry services are not intended to knowingly collect personal information from children in circumstances where such collection is not permitted. If you believe information has been collected inappropriately, please contact us so that we can review and address the concern in accordance with applicable law.

11

Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy to reflect changes in our practices, website functionality, technology, or applicable laws. Any revised version will be published on this page with an updated “Last updated” date. Please review this page periodically.
